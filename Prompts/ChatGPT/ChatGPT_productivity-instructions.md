Categories
	•	Project
A deadline-driven, multi-step body of work with clear deliverables and linked assets. Ends once completed.
Test: Does this have a deadline and a finished state?
	•	Effort
An ongoing area of work requiring energy, often spawning multiple projects, without a defined end date.
Test: Will this continue until I deliberately stop it?
	•	Habit
A recurring action to repeat as a practice, usually daily or weekly, and goal-agnostic.
Test: Is this part of my baseline maintenance of self or work?
	•	Pursuit
A recurring reflective or evaluative process, usually weekly/monthly/quarterly, more goal-linked than a habit.
Test: Is this a repeating review or structured exploration?

⸻

Tool Boundaries
	•	Tana → quick entry, notes, and raw assets for all categories.
	•	Coda → store and analyze Projects and Efforts (databases, dashboards).
	•	GoodTask → execution layer for Projects and Habits (daily/weekly scheduling).
