Grand Living Area (!Grand)

Create a bright, airy living space with Scandinavian beach influences. Emphasize white and light wood tones to evoke calm and simplicity, with touches of cool gray and sage green for contrast. The overall feel should be soft, natural, and uncluttered—ideal for relaxation, reading, and social connection.

⸻

Color Palette and Ratios

Design the space with white as the foundational base, light wood as the primary accent, and cool gray and sage as gentle supporting colors.

Color	Role	Suggested Ratio	Notes
White	Primary Color	50%	Walls, ceilings, and large furniture pieces; enhances light and space.
Light Wood	Secondary Color	30%	Used in flooring, tables, shelving, and accent furniture.
Cool Gray	Base Color	15%	Soft contrast on textiles, rugs, or side furniture; keeps things grounded.
Sage Green	Accent Color	5%	Subtle vibrancy through throw pillows, plants, or artwork.


⸻

Example Layout Based on Ratios
	•	Walls & Ceilings: Bright white to maximize light.
	•	Flooring: Light wood or light-toned laminate.
	•	Furniture: Clean lines, primarily white or light wood. Soft gray pieces provide contrast.
	•	Decor & Accents: Sage plants, linens, or ceramics to add warmth and variation.

⸻

Visual Harmony and Flexibility

This palette promotes a calm, beachy openness. Emphasize texture—linen, cotton, soft knits—rather than bold colors. Adjust the sage or gray usage if certain pieces stand out more strongly (like a large sage-toned sofa or rug), always aiming for balance and lightness.

⸻

This method ensures your living space remains minimal yet welcoming, with a timeless coastal tone.

Boho Beach Bedroom (!Bedroom)

Design a breezy, relaxed bedroom infused with boho beach charm. Focus on beige and neutral tones, light woods, and soft whites to build a restful foundation, with pale blue accents and organic textures for character. The mood should be peaceful and layered—perfect for winding down.

⸻

Color Palette and Ratios

The goal is a neutral-dominant color scheme with soft coastal hints.

Color	Role	Suggested Ratio	Notes
Beige	Primary Color	50%	Walls, bedding, curtains, and soft furnishings; provides warmth and softness.
White	Secondary Color	20%	Base layers—sheets, walls, or trim—to brighten and clean the space.
Light Wood	Base Color	20%	Furniture and accessories; keeps it earthy and natural.
Pale Blue	Accent Color	10%	Art, throw blankets, or pillows to introduce a breezy, oceanic feel.


⸻

Example Layout Based on Ratios
	•	Walls: Beige or off-white with soft texture (matte or limewash ideal).
	•	Flooring & Furniture: Light wood in bed frames, dressers, and side tables.
	•	Textiles: Layered whites and beiges; introduce pale blue in a throw or pillow.
	•	Decor & Accents: Rattan, woven baskets, pale blue prints, seashells or driftwood.

⸻

Visual Harmony and Flexibility

Keep the space soft and tactile. Layer textures like cotton, linen, and woven materials rather than introducing bold colors. If pale blue is too subtle, you can increase its presence in art or textile patterns while maintaining the room’s serenity.

⸻

This approach ensures your bedroom feels airy, calm, and personal—evoking relaxed coastal mornings and cozy evenings.
